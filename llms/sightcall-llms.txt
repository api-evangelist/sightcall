# SightCall: Remote Visual Support Platform

> SightCall VISION is an enterprise remote visual support platform that connects experts, technicians, and customers through live AR-enhanced video. Teams use it to diagnose and resolve issues in real time, capture frontline expertise as reusable knowledge, and apply AI-powered visual inspection, all without requiring on-site visits.

## Platform

- [Platform Overview](https://sightcall.com/platform/): SightCall VISION: the all-in-one visual AI platform for modern service teams, combining remote visual support, AI insights, and knowledge capture.
- [Remote Visual Support](https://sightcall.com/platform/remote-visual-support/): Live HD video with AR annotations, screen sharing, snapshot capture, remote camera control, and multi-party calls. No download required for customers via WebRTC browser link.
- [Insights](https://sightcall.com/platform/insights/): AI analyzes live sessions to surface trends, identify knowledge gaps, and help service leaders make faster, more informed decisions.
- [Xpert Knowledge](https://sightcall.com/platform/xpert-knowledge/): Automatically converts recorded sessions into structured, step-by-step multimedia tutorials. Scales institutional expertise to technicians and AI agents without extra effort from service teams.
- [Visual AI](https://sightcall.com/platform/visual-ai/): Computer vision for real-time defect detection, parts recognition, object classification, and automated data capture (OCR for serial numbers, gauges, meter readings).
- [Security & Compliance](https://sightcall.com/platform/security/): SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, GDPR, and CCPA compliance. Encrypted video transmission and storage, role-based access, SAML/OIDC SSO, and full audit traceability.
- [Trust Center](https://trust.sightcall.com/): Live compliance status and security documentation.

## Solutions

- [Field Service](https://sightcall.com/solutions/field-service/): Increase first-time fix rates, reduce truck rolls, and enable remote factory acceptance testing (FAT). Measured outcomes: +20% first call resolution, -50% truck rolls, -69% average resolution time.
- [Customer Service](https://sightcall.com/solutions/customer-service/): Level 1 and Level 2 support teams resolve issues remotely with live video and AR guidance, reducing average handle time and escalations.
- [Insurance](https://sightcall.com/solutions/insurance/): Remote loss adjustment, real-time damage assessment, and authenticated evidence collection with GPS and timestamp verification. Supports both live and asynchronous (SightCall Snap) workflows.
- [Telehealth](https://sightcall.com/solutions/telehealth/): HIPAA-compliant remote support for medical device installation, maintenance, and clinical assistance.
- [Field Service Savings Calculator](https://sightcall.com/field-service-savings-calculator/): Estimate ROI from reduced truck rolls and faster resolution times.
